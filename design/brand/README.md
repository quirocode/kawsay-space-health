# UPRIGHT brand assets

Name: **UPRIGHT** (uppercase in the wordmark, “Upright” in running prose).
Tagline: **Land on your feet.** · **Aterriza de pie.**
Promise: *know the body you land with.*

## Files
- `upright-mark.svg` — primary mark, cream tile. Use in the app nav, landing and decks.
- `upright-favicon.svg` — blue tile, no orbit. Use at 16–64 px and as the PWA icon.
- App copies: `public/upright-mark.svg`, `public/favicon.svg`.

## Mark
A body standing on a horizon, with an orbit crossing it: the landing-readiness test (stand up without fainting) plus the space mission. The terracotta line is the ground; the blue figure is the crew member.

- Minimum size: 24 px. Below that, use the favicon version without the orbit.
- Clear space: at least the diameter of the head circle on every side.
- On cream or photos, use the tiled mark. Do not place the blue figure directly on a dark photo.

## Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| NASA blue | `#0B3D91` | figure, orbit, primary tile |
| Cream | `#F7EFE8` | tile background, figure on blue |
| Terracotta | `#B5602D` | horizon / ground line |

## Don't
- Don't rotate, stretch, outline, gradient or add a drop shadow inside the mark.
- Don't change the ground line to blue or the figure to terracotta; the contrast is the point.
- Don't use the llama astronaut as the product logo. It stays as an optional mascot for the demo story.
