KAWSAY modifications: selected muscle meshes and selected heart, lung, digestive, brain and eye structures; reduced polygon counts; merged systems into two models; added names and KAWSAY body-zone metadata; applied runtime colors. No kidney or reproductive assets were included. Anatomical labels retain the source English terminology. The models are illustrative references; health assessments are calculated at the existing KAWSAY zone level.
