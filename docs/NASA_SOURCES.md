The CO₂ finding uses [NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 2 Rev. F, V2 6004](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/nasa-std-3001-vol-2.pdf) for the 3 mmHg habitat limit over one hour. KAWSAY computes a time-weighted average from timestamped samples. Complete coverage with a maximum 15-minute hold per sample is a prototype data rule. The guided mission, symptom logic and landing score use simulated inputs and prototype assumptions.
